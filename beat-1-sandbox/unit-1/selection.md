## Check rationale

The uploaded rubric's `Issue is bounded and actionable` check says: “Pass if the issue describes one concrete bug, documentation task, or narrowly scoped feature with enough behavior or acceptance detail to identify a first PR.” I chose this wording because a first contributor needs a change that can be understood and verified without designing an entire subsystem. The check also explicitly distinguishes a specific bug with multiple possible implementations from a true umbrella or undefined product request.

## Trade-offs

The rubric is intentionally conservative: it rejects active claims, archived/stale repositories, undefined scope, and explicit AI bans. This may reject an interesting old issue with a stale claim or a useful issue in a quiet repository, but those false negatives are safer for a first contribution than sending a newcomer into work that cannot be reviewed or accepted.
